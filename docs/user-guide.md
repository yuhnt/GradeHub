# User guide

Task Grading Hub is where teachers post tasks and students hand in their work
as a PDF before the deadline. Teachers then read and grade each submission.

All times are shown in your own time zone.

## For students

### Create your account

1. Open the site and choose **Create an account**.
2. Pick a username (your teachers will see it), enter your email and a
   password of at least 8 characters.
3. You are signed in straight away.

Teacher accounts are created by the school administrator, not on this page.

### Hand in a task

1. **Tasks** lists what is open, soonest deadline first. Each task shows where
   you stand: *To do*, *Submitted*, your grade, or *Not submitted* once closed.
2. Open a task, read the instructions, then under **Your submission** choose
   your PDF (or drag it onto the box) and press **Submit PDF**.
3. Only PDF files up to 10 MB are accepted. Export Word or Google Docs files
   as PDF first.

You can hand in **one file per task**. To change it before the deadline,
press **Delete** under your submission and upload the new file. After the
deadline nothing can be changed, and late work can't be uploaded.

### See your grade

Your grade (out of 100) and your teacher's feedback appear on the task page
and on **My submissions**, which also shows your average.

## For teachers

### Post a task

1. **Tasks → New task**.
2. Enter a title, the instructions and the deadline (in your time zone).
3. Students see the task immediately.

**Edit** changes a task at any time. Moving the deadline later reopens the
task for everyone who hasn't submitted; moving it earlier can lock students
out. Saving a task whose deadline has passed needs a new, future deadline.

**Delete** removes the task together with every submission, grade and PDF
for it. This can't be undone; the confirmation tells you how many
submissions will go.

### Try the task yourself

On your task's page, **Try it as a student** lets you upload sample PDFs as
often as you like, even after the deadline. Students never see these.

### Grade

1. Open your task. **Submissions** lists who handed in and when; switch the
   filter to **Not graded** to see what is left.
2. Press **Grade**. The PDF opens on the left (or use **Download PDF**).
3. Enter a whole number from 0 to 100 and, if you like, written feedback.
4. **Save grade**, or **Save and grade next** to move straight on.

You can change a grade at any time; the student sees the latest one.
**Export CSV** downloads all grades and feedback for the task for your
spreadsheet.

## Account help

- **Forgot your password?** On the sign-in page choose *Forgot password?*,
  enter your email and follow the link in the email. The link works once and
  only for a short time.
- **"Your session has expired"** means you were signed in for a long time
  (a day by default). Sign in again; nothing you saved is lost.
- **Signing out** on a shared computer removes everything about your account
  from that browser.
